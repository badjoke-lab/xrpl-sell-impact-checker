Methods by app

Each XSIC app has different assumptions. Use this page to map input fields to outputs, interpret values, and understand hard limits.

Sell Impact

  • Input: token pair, sell size, and market context from XRPL DEX/AMM snapshots.
  • Output: estimated execution impact, expected receive amount, and route context.
  • How to read: larger estimated impact usually means thinner available depth for the size entered.
  • Limits: estimates can diverge from final execution under fast market movement or path changes.

Liquidity Pulse

  • Input: tracked pair set and periodic depth/state snapshots.
  • Output: liquidity condition labels, comparative depth indicators, and changes over time.
  • How to read: use direction and persistence, not one isolated point, before changing execution plans.
  • Limits: coverage follows tracked markets and may miss untracked venues or short-lived events.

Flow Alert

  • Input: labeled exchange/whale flow history and recent snapshots.
  • Output: flow pressure indicators and directionally useful context.
  • How to read: alerts indicate notable shifts, not guaranteed future price moves.
  • Limits: wallet labeling, reporting delay, and chain activity spikes can affect interpretation.

Exposure Graph

  • Input: issuer and trustline relationship data from XRPL account states.
  • Output: network-style exposure structures and concentration cues.
  • How to read: focus on concentration pockets and dependency paths, not only node count.
  • Limits: structural exposure is not equivalent to immediate liquidity or trading intent.
