How to operate XSIC as a four-app decision console

Each app answers a different question. Recommended sequence is Sell Impact → Liquidity Pulse → Flow Alert → Exposure Graph. Use the full chain when execution size, timing, or confidence is material.

Recommended operating sequence

  • Step 1 / Sell Impact: estimate immediate execution outcome and route dependence for the planned size.
  • Step 2 / Liquidity Pulse: verify whether current depth posture supports the same assumptions beyond one snapshot.
  • Step 3 / Flow Alert: check transfer pressure regime that may alter book behavior between decision and execution.
  • Step 4 / Exposure Graph: confirm structural concentration risk around issuer/trustline topology before committing.

Sell Impact

  • What it watches: executable route state across orderbook and AMM paths at request time.
  • Primary input: token + issuer pair, side/size assumptions, and current market snapshot from connected XRPL endpoints.
  • Output: estimated receive amount, impact/slippage range, route composition, and confidence cues.
  • How to interpret: compare the estimate against internal size tolerance and note whether the route depends on thin or unstable liquidity segments.
  • Limits: estimate is snapshot-bound; rapid queue changes, path updates, and latency can widen realized divergence.

Liquidity Pulse

  • What it watches: depth posture and short-horizon persistence for tracked pairs and pools.
  • Primary input: rolling depth/price snapshots with app-level presets and window configuration.
  • Output: state labels, trend strips, deviation metrics, and freshness markers.
  • How to interpret: treat multi-window persistence as stronger evidence than a single print; adjust size or timing when posture is unstable.
  • Limits: only covered pairs/pools are represented; events between polling intervals may not be visible in real time.

Flow Alert

  • What it watches: exchange-linked and whale-linked transfer pressure over configured windows.
  • Primary input: labeled address flows, aggregate snapshots, and historical window data maintained by XSIC pipelines.
  • Output: pressure direction cues, alert states, and supporting context totals.
  • How to interpret: use alerts as regime context; confirm with Sell Impact and Liquidity Pulse before changing execution assumptions.
  • Limits: label coverage and ingestion delay can create temporary false positives, false negatives, or muted signals.

Exposure Graph

  • What it watches: issuer-account-trustline topology and concentration structure.
  • Primary input: ledger relationship state for selected issuer/accounts and trustline graph extraction.
  • Output: concentration patterns, dependency clusters, and intermediary concentration cues.
  • How to interpret: identify single points of structural dependency and concentration pockets that can amplify execution fragility.
  • Limits: structural concentration is not a fill guarantee signal; always pair with live impact/liquidity checks.

Estimated values vs observed values

  • Estimated values: model-derived projections from the latest readable state (for example expected receive or implied impact).
  • Observed values: recorded snapshots, flow aggregates, and graph structure that describe what has already been ingested.
  • Operational rule: when estimate and observed context disagree, reduce size, delay execution, or require manual review.
  • Decision hygiene: do not treat any single app as authoritative; confidence comes from cross-app alignment plus fresh timestamps.
