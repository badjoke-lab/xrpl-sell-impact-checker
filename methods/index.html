Methods

XSIC estimates sell impact by simulating fills against XRPL order books (CLOB) and AMM pools, then choosing the best venue for the requested trade size.

Data inputs

  • CLOB order books via XRPL book_offers.
  • AMM pool reserves and trading fee via XRPL amm_info.
  • Optional fiat rates (XRP → fiat) from CoinGecko.

Computation flow

  1. Validate token (currency + issuer) and requested sell amount.
  2. Fetch order book offers up to the limit you select.
  3. Simulate fills against the book, computing average price and slippage.
  4. Fetch AMM reserves and estimate the AMM swap output for the same amount.
  5. Pick the venue that yields the higher receive amount (CLOB or AMM).

Important limits

  • Order book depth is capped by the Orderbook limit; large trades can be under-estimated if liquidity exists deeper than the limit.
  • AMM estimates assume current pool reserves and do not include future swaps or governance actions.
  • Fiat values are informational and depend on the external rate feed availability.

How to read results

  • Sellability indicates whether the requested amount can fill.
  • Slippage estimates how much the average price worsens as size grows.
  • Max sell under X% shows the largest size that stays below a chosen impact threshold.
