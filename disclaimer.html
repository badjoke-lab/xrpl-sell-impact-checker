Disclaimer

XSIC is a technical decision-support console. Every app output must be handled as conditional information, not an execution promise.

1) Non-investment advice

XSIC content is informational and operational only. It is not financial, investment, legal, tax, accounting, or fiduciary advice, and does not create an advisory relationship.

2) Estimated values, not guarantees

Sell Impact estimates, Liquidity Pulse states, Flow Alert signals, and Exposure Graph patterns are analytical outputs derived from available data. They can diverge from realized fills, realized prices, or realized market behavior.

3) Upstream dependency and data quality

XSIC depends on XRPL endpoints and related upstream interfaces. If upstream data is delayed, missing, inconsistent, or unavailable, XSIC outputs can be stale, partial, degraded, or temporarily absent.

4) Latency, drift, and missing intervals

Between snapshots, market and ledger state may change. Queue updates, path updates, and ingestion delays can create drift between what XSIC shows and what is executable at action time.

5) User responsibility and risk control

Users remain solely responsible for validating freshness, cross-checking app disagreement, sizing risk, and deciding whether to execute, delay, reduce, or cancel actions.

6) Scope of responsibility

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, XSIC maintainers are not liable for direct or indirect losses arising from use of, reliance on, or inability to access the service, including losses related to stale data, endpoint failure, or interpretation error.
