FAQ

Quick answers to common questions about XSIC.

Why can’t I enter XRP alone?

XRPL XRP is the quote asset for most order books. XSIC estimates sell impact for IOUs against XRP, so it requires both a currency code and issuer. For XRP/XRP there is no order book to simulate.

How fresh is the data?

XSIC calls public XRPL endpoints in real time and records the fetch time shown in the results. If live data is unavailable, cached values may be shown with a warning.

Why do CLOB and AMM results differ?

CLOB results are based on visible offers, while AMM results depend on pool reserves and fees. XSIC compares both and uses the better outcome for your requested amount.

Is fiat pricing guaranteed?

No. Fiat amounts are optional and sourced from external feeds. If the feed is down, XSIC will still show XRP values and note that fiat is unavailable.
