How this works (methods)
  • Exposure in this app: concentration weight from absolute trustline balances in live account_lines data.
  • Bounded graph includes: issuer + top counterparties by exposure (fixed max for fast, stable rendering). It excludes long-tail counterparties from node display.
  • Overall Low / Medium / High / Unknown: derived from concentration score + observed issuer controls, then reduced when evidence is unknown; returns Unknown when issuer is invalid or one dimension fails.
  • Observed / Not observed / Unknown: derived from current account flag evidence. Unknown means the app could not verify that control from current data.

Exposure shows concentration structure. Risk shows issuer-control evidence from account flags.
