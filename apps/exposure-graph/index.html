How to read this view

  • Bounded graph: this view intentionally shows only issuer + top counterparties (fixed max) for fast live refresh and stable layout.
  • Exposure: absolute trustline balances from account_lines, treated as concentration weight by counterparty share.
  • Observed / Not observed / Unknown: issuer-control status from live account flags; unknown means the runtime could not confirm that control now.

Exposure shows concentration structure. Risk shows issuer-control evidence from account flags.
